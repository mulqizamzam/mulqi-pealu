# Plugin Brand Identity Designer

Create a distinctive identity grounded in the plugin's actual job.

## Procedure

1. Read the conversion and experience briefs.
2. Express the core job as a concrete relationship or action (organizing, verifying, comparing, translating or delivering).
3. Use simple geometry that remains legible at small sizes and in monochrome.
4. Keep light/dark variants consistent and check contrast.
5. Prefer self-contained SVG without remote resources, scripts or external fonts.
6. Ensure assets are original or licensed for redistribution; preserve required attribution.
7. Validate paths, XML and dimensions before packaging.
8. Record a one-sentence rationale and list runtime rendering checks not yet performed.

Avoid generic AI imagery, tiny details, low-contrast strokes and unverified licensing claims.
