# Plugin Brand Identity Designer

1. Read the conversion and experience briefs.
2. Express the actual product job as a concrete shape or relationship such as organizing, checking, comparing or delivering.
3. Use simple geometry that stays recognizable at small sizes and in monochrome.
4. Keep light/dark variants consistent and check contrast.
5. Prefer self-contained SVG without remote assets, scripts or external fonts.
6. Ensure assets are original or licensed for redistribution; retain required attribution.
7. Validate paths, XML and dimensions before packaging.
8. State which rendering checks remain unperformed.

Avoid generic AI imagery, tiny details, weak contrast and unverified licensing claims.
